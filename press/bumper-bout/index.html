The game

About Bumper Bout

Bumper Bout is a competitive PvP pinball game played across a joined, shared playfield. Each player defends their own drain while scoring points, building offensive opportunities and sending additional threats toward their opponent.

Distinct characters bring unique powers to the match, while stages introduce their own mechanics and ways to disrupt the rally.

What to expect

Key Features

  • Shared-playfield competitionA joined pinball-versus concept where both sides shape the same match.
  • 9+ charactersA varied roster, each with a unique super power.
  • 20+ stagesBespoke mechanics make each full-game stage play differently.
  • Local multiplayerHead-to-head play for two players on the same PC.
  • Solo playCPU opponents plus arcade and survival single-player modes.
  • Online multiplayerAn online head-to-head feature for the full game.

Demo note: Features above describe the announced full game. The Steam page is the source of truth for content available in the current demo.
