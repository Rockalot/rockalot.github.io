Bumper Bout

Bumper Bout is a 1–2 player local PvP pinball game.

  • A new joined pinball versus concept
  • 20+ stages, each with bespoke mechanics
  • 9+ characters, each with a unique super power
  • Local and online multiplayer
  • Arcade and survival single-player modes

Players engage in direct competition on a joined playfield with shared balls. Each player has five lives. Hitting bumpers and targets yields points that can be spent to fire Attack Balls at an opponent. Save four and they combine into a Super Ball, offering special benefits and activating each character’s unique super power.

Easy to play, hard to master.
Simple to pick up, impossible to put down.
