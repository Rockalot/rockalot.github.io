The current game

Competitive two-player pinball on a shared playfield.

Defend your own drain while sending balls into your opponent’s side. Score to create new offensive opportunities, master distinctive characters, and battle across colourful stages with their own surprises.

About Rockalot

Small studio.
Big arcade energy.

Rockalot is the independent game development studio of Rocco Loria, based in Brisbane, Australia. It creates colourful, approachable arcade-style games made to be picked up, shared and replayed.

Bumper Bout is currently in development and won the Game of the Show People’s Choice Award at Queensland Games Festival 2026.
